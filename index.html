About Skyward

Skyward Simulations is an independent flight simulation development studio founded in 2024 by Lucas Winter. Our mission is simple: to create highly detailed, authentic aircraft simulations that raise the standard of realism within the flight simulation industry.

The flight simulation community has long sought aircraft that go beyond surface-level accuracy. At Skyward Simulations, we believe that true immersion comes from meticulous attention to detail, deep technical research, and a commitment to recreating aircraft as faithfully as possible. Every project we undertake is driven by a passion for aviation and a desire to deliver products that both enthusiasts and professionals can appreciate.

To achieve this level of realism, we work directly with real-world pilots, engineers, and maintenance technicians throughout the entire development process. Their knowledge and experience help us validate aircraft behavior, system logic, operational procedures, and performance characteristics. In many cases, these industry professionals are involved before any modeling or coding work begins, allowing us to establish a solid foundation of technical accuracy from the very start.

Our research process is extensive. We spend countless hours studying aircraft flight manuals, maintenance documentation, wiring diagrams, system schematics, blueprints, operational procedures, and other technical references. It is not uncommon for our team to work through thousands of pages of documentation to fully understand how an aircraft operates before a single feature is implemented. This dedication ensures that every system behaves as realistically as possible and reflects the complexity of the real aircraft.

What sets Skyward Simulations apart is the direct involvement of every member of our team. Our developers are deeply engaged in each project, contributing ideas, expertise, and feedback throughout development. This collaborative environment allows us to continuously refine our products and maintain the highest standards of quality.

As we continue to grow, our goal remains clear: to become a trusted name in flight simulation by delivering aircraft that combine technical accuracy, immersive functionality, and exceptional attention to detail. We are committed to pushing the boundaries of what is possible in flight simulation and helping shape the future of the hobby we all enjoy.

We are always interested in connecting with individuals who share our passion for aviation and simulation. Whether you are a developer, pilot, engineer, technician, researcher, artist, or simply someone with a strong interest in creating the next generation of flight simulation products, we would be delighted to hear from you. The industry needs more passionate people dedicated to building truly authentic aircraft simulations, and we are always open to expanding our team with like-minded individuals who share our vision.
